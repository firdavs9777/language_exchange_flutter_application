**Summary**
- Real web breakages: Nearby is fully dead (wrong param names on both layers, wrong field names, a 404 route) and @username search 404s (wrong mount). Each needs a small fix in the web slice/component.
- The biggest cross-platform risk is on the backend: list/daily/topic payloads and the app's `getUser` ignore privacy toggles, and follow/public-profile ignore blocks. Web's profile page is actually *more* correct than the app here.
- Feature gaps: gatherings/clubs/voice rooms/language rooms, the Serious segment, smart sort, waves archive, wave-cap paywall and a full language catalog in filters. None of these findings are fixed in the 23 unpushed commits, and those commits also delete the community test suite.

# BananaTalk 2.7.0

Version 2.7.0 (10580). Follows 2.6.1 (10574), which is live. Android ships first. The manual steps are in `2.7.0-handoff.md`.

## New
- **First-week guides.** New members get a short guide on each tab for their first days (one conversation, one moment, one lesson, a complete profile). It retires as soon as they do the real thing ("Getting started · N of 4"). There is also a long-press tip under the first message they receive.
- **Launch pop-ups, one at a time.** At most one unrequested pop-up per launch, by priority: update → announcement → ads notice → feature spotlight. AI Study is now part of the spotlight rotation. New members in their first week see none of these, so their one pop-up can be the notification prompt when it is actually useful (first message, first match).
- **Announcements.** Announcements from the admin panel show as a launch pop-up, a notification row and a push, each opening a detail screen at `/announcement/:id`. They can be targeted by language. There is a Settings switch to turn them off.
- **Full localization.** Every screen is translated in all 19 languages (chat, moments, reels, stories, community, AI Study, Learn, VIP, coins, settings, calls, errors). Relative times follow the app language. Tajik no longer crashes on dates or member counts.
- **Calls, Phase 2:**
  - Decline with a message (two quick replies or your own). You are told if the message could not be delivered.
  - Missed-call notification with Call back and Message buttons.
  - "Allow calls" switch. Quiet hours hold calls when "Allow urgent" is off, and the quiet-hours window uses your phone's time zone.
  - Turn the camera on during a voice call.
  - Draggable, swappable self-view.
- **Calls, Phase 1 (reliability):**
  - Calls stay alive in the background (Android foreground service).
  - The full-screen incoming call is requested on Android 14+.
  - There is one ring per call.
  - The Calls list in the Chat tab has a missed badge, and call history shows in conversations.
- **VIP on Android:** "Manage Subscription" opens Google Play's subscription page, which is where a Play subscription is cancelled.

## Fixes (audit of 2026-10-13)
- **Rejoining a call after the app was closed.**
  - Your camera never turns on by itself, and the speaker is not forced on.
  - A missed accept followed by a video upgrade connects as video.
  - When the other person turns their camera on, your audio stays where it was and the screen stays awake.
- **Declining with a message.** The message counts characters the way the server does, so a decline with emoji always goes through. Korean and Japanese input is no longer cut mid-word.
- **Signing out and in again.** A second account signed in without restarting now gets its own launch pop-up.
- **Count wording.** Club and gathering counts show the right number in Tagalog, Russian and other languages where the "one" plural form also covers numbers other than 1.
- **Android pushes.** Notifications received in the background now pop up as banners, through the app's own channel instead of "Miscellaneous". The status-bar icon is a proper monochrome icon.
- **Android web links.**
  - Links to moments, profiles, community and invites open the app.
  - Privacy and terms links stay in the browser.
  - The moment link from the save sheet now opens the app too.
- **Android hygiene.**
  - Tajik appears in system App languages, and native screens mirror for Arabic.
  - Bluetooth, location and Wi-Fi are no longer required to install the app.
  - Removed unused permissions: contacts, phone state, overlay, battery exemption, media library.
  - Removed dead Facebook SDK entries.
  - Incoming-call components can only be triggered by BananaTalk itself.
  - No duplicate missed-call notification.
  - Backups no longer copy the login session.

# BananaTalk 2.6.0

**Theme:** The monetization layer — coins that buy visibility, ads that unlock limits, VIP that means something.

## What's new (visible only when the server enables it — ships dark)
- **Profile Boost (150 coins / 24h)** — be the sixth daily match for everyone whose languages fit you, and pinned at the top of Partners; see how many people saw you. Capacity-limited (≤10 per 1,000 monthly actives) so a boost always delivers (`boostsEnabled`).
- **+3 more matches (40 coins)** — extend today's batch from 6 to 9 cards.
- **See who waved / who viewed you (60 coins / 24h, or VIP)** — new builds ask for the gated list; older builds keep today's behaviour.
- **Watch an ad to unlock** — at a feature limit, a rewarded ad grants the same unit a paid unlock would, up to 5 per feature per day; one interstitial per session at natural pauses (never for VIP) (`rewardedLimitsEnabled`).
- **VIP, repriced and honest** — $3.99/month, $24.99/year (save 48%); perks shown are the perks enforced: no ads, unlimited daily matches, see who waved/viewed, unlimited waves, advanced filters.
- **Boost receipt** — a push when your boost ends with how many people saw you.

## Fixes
- Store "Save 40%" and the fake yearly original price on the plans endpoint replaced by real numbers.
- VIP benefits list no longer advertises "Priority support" (nothing enforced it).

## Server flags this build understands
`boostsEnabled`, `rewardedLimitsEnabled`, `rewardedFeatures` (new) plus everything from 2.5.0. All default off; the app behaves exactly like 2.5.0 until they are turned on.

## Rollout (Task C9)
1. Backend tranche C merged + deployed dark.
2. Store pricing changed in App Store Connect / Play Console: monthly 3.99, yearly 24.99 (quarterly left as-is; it is hidden in the app).

# BananaTalk 2.6.1

Supersedes 2.6.0 (never submitted). Everything in `2.6.0.md` (and 2.5.0) plus:

## New (dark until the server enables it)
- **Wave limit dialog** — when the server's wave cap is on (`waveCapEnabled`) and a member runs out of daily waves, they get "one more wave for 10 coins / watch an ad" instead of a dead-end error. Keyed on the server's `wave_cap` code, so ordinary rate limits keep today's message.

## Fixes
- Notification taps during cold start now reliably land on the requested tab.
- Invite codes are kept when referrals are temporarily switched off, and stored for social sign-ups who haven't finished their profile.
- The rewarded-unlock and limit dialogs are fully translated (no more English "Maybe Later" in other languages).
- A chat edge case where a just-unlocked message could skip the limit check twice.
- If app settings failed to load during sign-up, the app retries once before choosing the first screen.

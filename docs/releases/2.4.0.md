# 2.4.0 (build 10571)

Previous release: 2.3.0+10570. Backend at `ca7ceda`, deployed on push to main.

**This release needs a content seed after deploy.** Vocabulary packs, grammar
units and exam vocabulary live in the database, so the content below is
invisible to learners until Actions → "Seed learning content" is run. The
code deploy alone does not publish it.

## What users get

### The app now speaks your language back to you

Fourteen languages had **no vocabulary content at all**, so their learners were
quietly served English words while trying to learn Japanese, Spanish or Thai.
The app said the lessons were in English; it still meant the vocabulary station
was teaching the wrong language.

- **1,020 new words** across Japanese, Spanish, Chinese, French, German,
  Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Turkish, Arabic
  and Hindi — beginner and intermediate, twenty words a pack.
- **42 grammar units**, three per language, in a real sequence rather than
  whatever the generator last produced.
- **Exam vocabulary for seven languages.** German had none at all despite
  Goethe being an offered exam; Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese
  and Chinese each had a single topic. All now cover A1 and B1.

### You can tell which answer you got wrong

The vocabulary test showed a score and nothing else — a wrong answer looked
exactly like a right one, so the station marked you without teaching you.
Answers are now marked individually, with the correct option and an
explanation.

### Listening clips play

The listening station was silent on iPhone. It now claims the audio session
before playing, so the ring/silent switch and any earlier voice call no longer
leave it mute.

### 모임 / Gatherings

- **Hosts can edit** a gathering — fix a typo, move the time, change the size —
  instead of cancelling and re-creating, which stranded everyone who had said
  yes. Attendees are notified when the time moves.
- **Hosts can admit or decline** join requests. The endpoint existed; the host
  was never told anyone was waiting.
- **My Gatherings** on your own profile, so a host can find their own gathering
  again after it scrolls out of the community tab.
- Gatherings show **where the host is** and the start time in your own timezone.

### Matching knows why you are here

The app has always been described as "Learn, Meet or Date" and has never
modelled it: a learner wanting grammar practice and someone wanting a date were
ranked by one identical formula.

You can now say what you are here for, and matching ranks accordingly. It only
ranks — nobody is hidden from anybody, and a profile with nothing set sees
exactly what it saw before.

## Notes for review

- **Nothing is filtered or excluded by the new intent field.** It adjusts
  ordering only. A user who sets nothing is unaffected.
- **"Open to dating" is never displayed.** It influences ranking and is not
  shown on any profile, in any response, to anyone.
- **Under-18 accounts cannot hold the dating intent.** It is removed when saved
  and ignored when scoring — two independent layers.
- No new permissions are requested. Nothing new is gated behind payment.

## Fixed

- Three crashes from `setState(() => x = future)` — the arrow evaluates to the
  assigned Future, which `setState` rejects. It was firing on pull-to-refresh
  in the gatherings tab and on **every host action** in the gathering and club
  detail screens.
- A layout overflow in the community moments empty and error states.
- A 61-pixel card overflow on a 320pt phone when a gathering had a long
  language label, found by a new test rather than in the wild.
- Dates on gathering cards read `16/9`, which is ambiguous across the 19
  locales the app ships in; they are now formatted per locale.

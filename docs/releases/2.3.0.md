# 2.3.0 (build 10570)

Previous release: 2.2.4+10560. Backend at `3b43e74`, already deployed — every
endpoint this build calls has been live and serving 401s to unauthenticated
callers since it shipped.

## What users get

The Study Hub's daily drop becomes a **daily learning pack**: four short
stations under a weekly theme, instead of two multiple-choice cards.

- **Four stations a day.** New vocabulary Monday–Thursday, a theme wrap quiz on
  Friday, a translation exercise at the weekend — plus grammar, listening and
  review every day. Around seven minutes.
- **A weekly theme.** Each week draws on one vocabulary pack, so Monday's words
  are Thursday's examples. Every level now has 25 themed weeks.
- **Grammar that starts where you are.** A 60-second placement sets your level,
  then you work through a real syllabus one unit at a time — 120 authored
  units across A1, A2, B1 and B2. Production had no B2 content at all before
  this.
- **Words you save come back.** New vocabulary enters spaced repetition
  automatically and returns in the review station a couple of days later.
- **Spoken listening clips**, and **AI-graded translation** with feedback and a
  suggested answer.
- **Progress worth opening.** Words mastered, learning and due; grammar units
  by section; listening accuracy; a 30-day calendar; and a weekly report of
  what you actually did.

Available in all 19 languages the app supports.

## Notes for review

- Nothing is gated behind payment; no new permissions are requested.
- Listening audio is generated server-side and cached, so repeated plays of the
  same sentence cost nothing extra.
- A learner with no vocabulary yet, or whose content is still being prepared,
  sees an honest empty state rather than a blank screen.
